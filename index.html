oblique.cards

A single prompt for the moments when work or life has quietly stalled.

Each card is a lens, not an instruction. It will not tell you what to do. It shifts the angle you are looking from, so the thing you are stuck on has somewhere to move. Draw one, sit with it, and share it if it lands.
