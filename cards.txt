# oblique.cards

# One card per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored,
# so you can use # freely to leave notes or group the deck into sections.
#
# To add a card:    add a line.
# To remove a card: delete its line.
# Then commit and let the deploy publish it. You never touch the app to do this.
#
# Shared links use a card's position in the deck (oblique.cards/?n=7).
# Comments and blank lines do not count towards that position, only real cards do.
# Append new cards at the end so existing shared links keep pointing at the
# same card. Reordering or deleting earlier lines will shift every link after it.

# --- carried over ---
Ask the question the work is avoiding.
Whose permission are you waiting for?
What is the honest version you're embarrassed by?
Which part are you protecting, and why?
Name the thing you keep not deciding.
What are you pretending not to know?
Assume you're wrong and proceed anyway.
Do the opposite and see who complains.
Choose the option with the better story.
What would this look like if it were easy?
Make the decision a coin could make.
Find the one part that scares you and enlarge it.
Stop before you're finished.
Do less, but finish.
Say the quiet objection out loud.
Find where you are being polite, and stop.
Honour the constraint you resent most.
Reverse the thing you assumed was fixed.
Keep the draft. Throw away the plan.
Say it once, then delete the explanation.
What if the flaw is the feature?
Ask the work what it wants to be.
Whose voice is that? Turn it down.
Do it badly and quickly, then look again.
Give it a deadline of ten minutes.
The obstacle is the shape of the answer.
What would the lazy version be? Try it.

# --- wanting ---
What did you want before you were told to want it?
Whose career are you copying, and are they happy?
What are you calling realistic so you do not have to want more?
You are allowed to want something you have no proof you can do.
What are you postponing until you feel qualified to want it?
What would you attempt if this did not have to define you?

# --- staying or leaving ---
Would you take this job again today?
What are you staying for? Is it still there?
You are not trapped. You are choosing.
Picture five years of exactly this.
Leaving is not the risk. Staying might be.
Staying is a decision. You just have not made it out loud.
What are you owed here that you will never be paid?
The door you think is locked. Have you tried it?
Quit the part, not the whole.

# --- the job hunt, and the no ---
Apply for the one you are not ready for.
A no tells you where they end, not where you do.
Stop shrinking yourself to fit the role.
Send it before you are ready. Ready is a story.
What are you auditioning for that you do not even want?
Who are you when no one is hiring?

# --- after the role goes ---
Who are you on the days you make nothing?
The title was theirs. What is yours?
What did the job let you avoid?
Grieve it, then rob it for parts.
The structure is gone. What were you only doing because of it?
What part of you got smaller to fit the job?

# --- reinvention ---
The expertise you are proud of might be the cage.
What are you too senior to try? Try it.
What would the beginner in you try that you now refuse?
Notice what you would do for free.
Who told you this was the only way up?

# --- the inner critic ---
Everyone competent feels like this.
Whose standard are you failing? Did you agree to it?
Behind whose schedule?
What if they hired you on purpose?

# --- deciding ---
You already know. Stop collecting opinions.
Both roads are fine. The waiting is the cost.
You are treating a reversible choice as permanent.
You are researching to avoid deciding.
Your list of pros and cons already knows the answer.
The thing you keep calling bad timing is the decision.
You keep asking how. The real question is whether.

# --- overwork and boundaries ---
What are you proving, and to whom?
Whose emergency is this?
Busy is a hiding place.
Say no to the good thing.
What falls apart when you leave on time?
What would you drop if no one noticed for a month?
Write the resignation you will never send.

# --- money against meaning ---
Enough is a number. Name it.
What is the salary actually for?
What would you do for half the money and twice the meaning?
Name the price of the thing you say money cannot buy.
Say the number out loud.

# --- ambition against contentment ---
Whose finish line are you running towards?
You are climbing well. Is it the right wall?
More was the plan. Is it still the point?
What if you already arrived, and forgot to notice?
Picture being content. Notice the flinch.

# --- work as identity ---
What is left when the work is taken away?
Who would you be with nothing to show for it?
What did you love before it became a career?
If the work disappeared, who would still know you?
Name the job you are actually doing, not the one on the contract.

# --- the general stuckness ---
Who benefits from you believing you have no options?
What are you being loyal to that is not loyal to you?
You stopped noticing what you tolerate.
You would never let a friend accept this.
Stop asking if you can. Ask if you want to.
Comfortable is also a decision.
Assume they already said no.
What are you waiting to be given that you could take?
